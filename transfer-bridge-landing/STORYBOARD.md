# Part 1 — 60-Second Product Reveal Storyboard
### Smart Inflatable Seat-Lift & Side Transfer Bridge · Version V2

**Tuned for Google Veo 3 / Sora.** Both generate best from ~8-second, single-continuous-shot,
natural-language prompts with explicit *camera*, *lighting*, *material*, and *audio* cues. So the
60s is built as **8 clips × ~7.5s**, each a self-contained prompt you generate separately and cut
together. Veo 3 renders native audio — the `AUDIO:` line is part of the prompt, not a separate step.

**Global style token** (paste at the end of every prompt for consistency):
> *Ultra-premium medical-hardware product film. Matte-black anodized surfaces, brushed aluminium,
> cyan pneumatic accent light (#17E0FF). Pure black seamless studio void, volumetric haze,
> high-contrast rim lighting, shallow depth of field, 50mm–100mm macro feel, cinematic color grade,
> 8K, photoreal, no text, no people unless stated.*

## EDITING NOTES
- **Cut on the pneumatic hits** — let the inflation (Clip 3) and the layer-locks (Clip 4) land on beats.
- **Grade:** crush blacks, lift only the cyan; keep everything else near-monochrome so #17E0FF is the only saturated colour in the film.
- **Pace:** Clips 1–2 slow (tension), 4–5 are the "sick" centrepiece (precise, weightless), 6–8 resolve.
- Veo 3 clips top out ~8s; if a beat needs to be longer, generate two takes and crossfade on a light sweep.
